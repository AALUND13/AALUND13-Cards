# AALUND13 Extra Picks Cards [v1.3.1]
AALUND13 Extra Picks Cards introduces <b>13</b> cards developed by <b>AALUND13</b>.  
If you encounter any bugs, please report them in the [issues](https://github.com/AALUND13/AALUND13-Cards/issues) tab.
<h3>Cards:</h3>
<b>Extra Cards:</b>

| Name | Rarity | Description | Stats |
|---|---|---|---|
| Card Factory | Legendary | Create Any Card at the start of each round. 30% chance the factory creates a Defective Card | 
| Cursed Theft | Legendary | Gain an extra pick only with 3 draws of other players cards. You steal the chosen card, but gain a Curse | 
| Corrupting Picks | Legendary | You get an extra pick each pick phase, but your hand slowly gets corrupted by the Void. | +1 Pick per round<br>+2.5% Unknow per pick
| Corruption Reflection | Epic | Gain a corrupted copy of each card in additional to the original, if available | 
| Picks Party | Exotic | Give yourself +2 queue picks at the cost of giving all enemies +1 queue pick<br>Queue picks will trigger once it is your turn | +2 Picks<br>+1 Enemies Pick
| Roll Of The Dice | Uncommon | Roll a number from 1 to 6. Higher numbers result in better stats. | 
| Cursed Knowledge | Exotic | Gain 3 <b>Cursed draws</b>. When you pick a card from a <b>Cursed draws</b>, you get a curse card alongside the regular card | +3 <b>Cursed Draws</b>
